Jack Marsden

I'm a biostatistician at the Matilda Centre for Research in Mental Health and Substance Use, University of Sydney.

I'm interested in how we can better support young people's mental health and prevent harm from alcohol and other drugs. Much of my work looks at prevention and treatment programs: whether they help, what makes them work, and why some people engage with them or benefit more than others. I predominantly work on the statistical side of those questions: figuring out how to measure change, understand patterns over time, and check how much our conclusions depend on the way we analyse the data.

Also sometimes football.
